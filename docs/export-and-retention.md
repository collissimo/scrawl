# Video export, retention and shrinking

Read this before changing `src/export/`, `src/retention.rs`, `helper/Export.swift` or `helper/Media.swift`.

## Video export (`src/export/`, `helper/Export.swift`)

`scrawl export <bundle>` writes one H.264/HEVC + AAC `.mp4`; the `videoExport` flag (default off) only gates the menu's **Export Last Capture as Video…** (→ `~/Movies/Scrawl`, progress beside the menu bar icon). Rust decides everything and the helper renders it from a JSON plan (AVAssetReader → Core Image crop/camera → Core Graphics ink → AVAssetWriter): `crop.rs` (`--crop auto|full|x,y,w,h` — auto is `autocrop` `Scope::Whole` — even-sided output size, audio window), `ink.rs` (strokes via `sketch::freehand_outline`, laser, pins, ripples; sizes mirror `render::annotate_keyframe_in_place`), `camera.rs` (`--zoom`: pure events → camera rects, critically damped springs, never zooms during fast cursor travel; derived from ScreenDemon's click zoom). Everything in the plan is on the video's clock (bundle time − `videoOffsetMs`); Mac `audio.wav` starts `videoOffsetMs` in, an iPhone movie uses its own audio. Output is constant frame rate (screen movies only have frames on change). Test with `cargo test export::`. On Windows `run_helper` renders the plan in process instead (`src/win/export/`, see `docs/windows.md`); keep the plan's JSON and `ExportInk`'s drawing in step on both sides.

## Retention and shrinking (`src/retention.rs`, `Media.shrink` in `helper/Media.swift`)

The app's `retention` worker (utility QoS) runs a pass 90 s after launch and daily: heavy media (`handoff::HEAVY_MEDIA`) to the Trash (the Recycle Bin on Windows, which frees the space only once it's emptied) after `mediaDays` (default 7, writes `.media-cleaned`), whole bundles after `bundleDays` (30); `"retention"` in `~/.config/scrawl/config.json`, Settings › Storage. It skips bundles under a day old or whose folder changed (ctime) in the last day, live `.media-pending`/`.media-sending` markers, `.pinned`, `.deliver.json`, and bundles `retention::claim`ed. Cabled-iPhone `recording.mov` (> 6 Mbps) is re-encoded to HEVC at 1600 px a minute after `finalize_live_capture`, written beside the bundle and renamed over the original; sample times are kept, so `scrawl.json` is unchanged. Anything reading `video` must handle it missing (`retention::missing_media_reason`). Try passes on a temp folder with `scrawl clean --captures DIR --days-ahead N [--dry-run]`.

**Delete the video after each capture** (Settings › Storage, `"keepVideo": false` in `"retention"`, default `true`): `retention::after_capture`, called once a capture is finished (`ui.rs`'s finish path, `scrawl record`; frames, contact sheet, `summary.md`, `scrawl.json` and the transcript are written by then), marks the bundle `.drop-video` and the worker deletes its video for good (no Trash: the point is the space) a minute later; `scrawl record` deletes it right away. `.media-cleaned` gets `"afterCapture": true`, so `missing_media_reason` names the setting. A bundle with `.deliver.json`, a live `.media-pending`/`.media-sending` or a claim keeps the video until a later pass (`Action::DeleteVideo`, after the bundle settled 10 minutes); a `.pinned` one keeps it. Only marked bundles lose their video, so switching it on never deletes older videos, and switching it off drops the marks. `audio.wav` stays (re-transcribing needs it) and `bundleDays`/`mediaDays` still apply to the rest. Without the video, `scrawl zoom`, `scrawl export` and `scrawl reprocess` stop with that reason, and `scrawl frames` falls back to the keyframes on disk; the review window only shows the contact sheet and keyframes.
